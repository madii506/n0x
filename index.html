01 — Every position is public

On Hyperliquid, everyone can see your trade.

Every perps account on Hyperliquid is an open book: size, side, entry, leverage and the exact price where it gets liquidated. Trackers follow the deposit back to the wallet that funded it, and from there to everything else you own.

  • Liquidation hunts. A big position with a visible liq price is a target. Price gets pushed to it.
  • Copy and front-run. Your entries get mirrored and your exits get sold into.
  • Your whole history. One linked deposit ties every wallet and every past trade to the same name.

02 — n0x breaks the link

Same trade. Same venue. Nobody’s name on it.

n0x opens your position from a brand-new Hyperliquid account. It is funded through NEAR Intents, so the money arrives from a solver, not from you. The position is still public. What’s gone is the line back to your wallet.

08 — Honest limits

Private isn’t invisible. Here’s the difference.

n0x removes the onchain link between your wallet and your position. It doesn’t make the position disappear, and it can’t stop someone matching amounts and times.

  • Vary amounts and timing. Don’t fund 4.20 SOL and open a $4.20k long a minute later.
  • Don’t reconnect the dots. Never send from the fresh account back to your main wallet.
  • One account per idea. Accounts are free. Make a new one whenever you like.
  • Back up the key. It exists only in this browser until you save it.
  • It’s still perps. Leverage can liquidate you. n0x is a client: it never holds funds and can’t reverse a trade.
  • Not a mixer. The money moves through a public swap network. You get no link, not anonymity.

09 — FAQ

Questions

Does n0x hold my money?

No. The fresh account’s key is created and kept in your browser. n0x’s server only relays public data and actions you’ve already signed. It can’t move funds.

Who can see my trades?

Anyone, like every Hyperliquid account. What they can’t see is which wallet funded it, because the USDC arrives from a NEAR Intents solver and not from you.

What is the private route?

A NEAR Intents 1Click swap. You send SOL to a one-time deposit address, and a solver pays USDC from its own balance into your fresh account’s Hyperliquid trading balance.

What if the route fails?

If a swap can’t complete before its deadline, NEAR Intents refunds the SOL to the wallet that sent it. You can follow the status live in the terminal.

Which markets can I trade?

Every live Hyperliquid perp market, from BTC, ETH and SOL to HYPE and the long tail. Leverage goes up to each market’s own maximum.

Does n0x take a fee?

No. You pay the route fee shown in the quote and Hyperliquid’s normal trading and withdrawal fees.

What if I clear my browser?

The key goes with it. Use “Back up key” right after you make an account, and “Import key” to bring it back on any device.

Is this a mixer?

No. Nothing is pooled. It’s a normal cross-chain swap that pays into a new account. It removes the direct link, but it doesn’t promise anonymity.
